03

Wix · Salesforce · Zapier

Marketing attribution & CRM automation for Wix

The client needed a scalable landing-page lead flow for paid campaigns, but the native Wix form setup could not capture hidden attribution data such as UTM values and GCLID, lacked custom submission logic, and did not support the automated lead routing required for a high-intent funnel. The business also needed every submission to flow into Salesforce CRM in real time.

Wix VeloJavaScriptWix CMSZapierWebhooksSalesforce
  • Built a custom Wix Velo form and CMS-backed storage layer to capture every lead with structured campaign data.
  • Captured 10+ attribution fields per submission, including UTM parameters, GCLID, landing page URL, timestamps, and campaign source details.
  • Connected the flow to Zapier webhooks for instant notifications and automated Salesforce lead creation with field mapping and real-time sync.
